Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to applications developed and published by AMOmarket, including but not limited to InvoiceFlex and DocuFlex.

Different applications may provide different features. Therefore, not every type of information described below is processed by every application. The information collected depends on the specific application and the features you choose to use.

Information We Collect

Depending on the application, we may process:

  • Email address (when signing in with Google or Email)
  • User account identifier provided by Firebase Authentication
  • Business profile information
  • Client information
  • Product information
  • Invoices and invoice-related data
  • PDF documents and document metadata
  • Camera access (DocuFlex document scanning)
  • Images selected by the user
  • Files imported or exported by the user

Authentication

InvoiceFlex supports Google Sign-In and Email Sign-In using Firebase Authentication. Authentication is used only to identify your account and enable cloud synchronization.

Cloud Synchronization

If you choose to use Cloud Sync, your application data may be securely synchronized using Google Firebase services. Cloud synchronization is optional and may require a Premium subscription depending on the application.

Cloud data may include:

  • Business profile
  • Clients
  • Products
  • Invoices
  • Application settings required for synchronization

Local Storage

Applications may store information locally on your device in order to provide offline functionality. For example:

  • Invoices
  • Business information
  • Clients
  • Products
  • PDF documents
  • OCR results
  • Signatures
  • Document edits

Advertising

Free versions of our applications may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may process limited device information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

Some applications may offer Premium features through Google Play Billing. All payment processing is handled securely by Google Play. AMOmarket does not receive or store your payment card information.

Data Sharing

AMOmarket does not sell or rent your personal information. We only share information with trusted service providers required to operate our applications, including:

  • Google Firebase
  • Google Play
  • Google AdMob

Data Security

We use commercially reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Data Retention

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it. Cloud data remains associated with your account until you remove it or delete your account, where applicable.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, update or delete your personal information. You may also stop using Cloud Sync at any time.

Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes become effective immediately after publication on this page.
