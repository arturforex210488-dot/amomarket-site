Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

AMOmarket respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how DocuFlex and other AMOmarket applications handle data.

Information We Collect

DocuFlex does not require account registration and does not collect personal documents or document content.

The application may access:

  • Camera (document scanning)
  • Photos and media (importing images)
  • Files and storage (opening, editing, exporting documents)

Local Processing

PDF files, OCR text recognition, signatures, images, annotations and document editing are processed locally on the user's device whenever possible.

Advertising

The free version of DocuFlex may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Advertising partners may process limited device information as described in their own privacy policies.

In-App Purchases

DocuFlex may offer optional Premium features through Google Play Billing. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play.

Data Sharing

AMOmarket does not sell, rent or share your documents, OCR text, signatures or PDF content with third parties.

Security

Users remain responsible for protecting access to their devices and files.
